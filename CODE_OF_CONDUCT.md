# IRIFE Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in IRIFE to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive for people with different backgrounds, identities, languages, abilities, and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and remain open to correction.
- Welcome questions and beginner contributions.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Use inclusive, professional language in issues, reviews, discussions, and shared spaces.
- Give clear attribution and respect intellectual property and licenses.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted advances.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Impersonation, plagiarism, or knowingly misrepresenting work.
- Unauthorized access, destructive testing, credential theft, or exposing secrets.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner/maintainers through GitHub. Do not post sensitive details or personal information in a public issue. Include relevant links and a concise description; reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in IRIFE repository spaces, pull requests, issues, discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing the project.

By participating, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.
